# PicSecure Renew Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 August 2026

PicSecure Renew is a renewal tracking application designed to help users track domains, subscriptions, services, documents, and other renewal dates.

## Information We Process

PicSecure Renew may process the following information when you choose to use its features:

* Account information provided through Firebase Authentication, such as your email address and account identifier.
* Renewal details that you create, including titles, providers, renewal dates, categories, notes, and reminder preferences.
* Thought notes and related content entered by you.
* Application preferences and synchronization status.
* Firebase and Google Cloud usage information requested through the configured monitoring service.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

* Provide renewal tracking and reminder features.
* Synchronize your vault between your signed-in devices.
* Restore your data when you sign in on another authorized device.
* Display Firebase and Google Cloud usage information requested by you.
* Maintain the security, reliability, and functionality of the application.

PicSecure Renew does not sell personal information, serve advertisements, or use personal information for behavioural advertising.

## Data Storage and Synchronization

Renewal information may be stored locally on your device. When cloud synchronization is enabled, applicable information is stored in Google Firebase services associated with the application.

Access to synchronized user records is protected using Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules intended to restrict each user to their own records.

Local backup files are created or restored only when requested by the user.

## Monitoring Credentials

Google Cloud service-account private keys and similar backend credentials must never be stored in the mobile application, desktop application, public repository, or browser storage. Such credentials are processed only by the configured backend service.

## Third-Party Services

PicSecure Renew may use:

* Google Firebase Authentication
* Google Cloud Firestore
* Google Analytics, when enabled
* Firebase Performance Monitoring, when enabled
* Google Cloud Monitoring
* GitHub Releases for application distribution and update information

These services may process limited technical or account information according to their respective privacy policies.

## Data Sharing

PicSecure Renew does not sell or rent user data. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above only as necessary to provide the requested application functionality or when required by law.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until it is deleted through the application, cleared from application storage, or the application is removed.

Cloud-synchronized information remains until it is deleted by the user or the associated account and cloud records are removed. Users may request deletion of their synchronized account data by contacting the maintainer.

## Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect stored and transmitted information. However, no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Users should protect their account, PIN, device credentials, backup files, and connected service credentials.

## Children’s Privacy

PicSecure Renew is not specifically directed to children under 13, and the maintainer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or service integrations change. The latest version will be published in this repository with an updated effective date.
